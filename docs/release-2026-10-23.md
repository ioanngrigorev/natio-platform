## Daily perspective follow-up

The greeting now shows one quote per local calendar day. Attribution is aligned right and links directly to its source; the refresh button and Library entry are removed, and the old library page redirects to Overview. The source corpus remains available internally.

`GET /admin/daily-perspective` resolves only the authenticated connection IP accepted by Fastify's configured trusted-proxy chain. The server queries ipapi.is over HTTPS, returns only city/country/IANA time zone, caches a hashed lookup for 24 hours, and uses a 1.5-second timeout. Private/reserved addresses are excluded. Device time is the explicit fallback; IP geography is approximate and may identify a VPN exit. No precise coordinates are returned or stored. The anonymous provider budget is capped at 25 new lookups per server process per UTC day, below its 30-request allowance; larger installations can configure `GEOLOCATION_API_KEY`. Rate-limit responses stop further lookups until the next UTC day. The greeting never blocks financial data or navigation. Source: https://ipapi.is/free-tier.html and https://ipapi.is/terms.html.

## Delivery timeline refinement

Pastel phase fills with readable dark labels, a neutral summary bar, exact dates beneath each phase and no clipped phase-name fragments. The default quarter view shows complete multiweek workstreams. Known untouched synthetic modules use 28/35/42 calendar-day illustrative schedules with parallel analysis/build and build/QA work; no real task dates are stretched. The seed migration is idempotent, records its revision, and skips user-edited specifications.

At the user’s subsequent visual review, dependency connector lines, their visibility toggle and the arrow legend were removed from Gantt. Compact predecessor text links remain available. Stored dependencies, backend cycle checks and blocked-start checks are unchanged; dates are not rescheduled automatically.
